# Prompt: Erklärvideo „Vom Gründer, der alles macht, zum Produkt im Markt“

Produce a **calm, documentary-style explainer film of 85 seconds** for **Fokus Innovation**, a small agency from Berlin. Fokus Innovation brings new products from Germany and Europe to market: it makes them visible, sells them with a system and connects the people behind them. The film tells **one story**: a founder who takes care of everything. The product is excellent, but the market does not know it yet. Fokus Innovation brings focus, and the product reaches its customers. The story follows the same steps as the homepage, which also opens with these six images.

Generate **every scene separately**. Put the style block below in front of every scene prompt. Generate **clean plates only**: no lettering, no logos, no user interfaces with readable text. All text, the logo and the subtitles are added in editing.

## Style block (in front of every scene, copy it verbatim)

```
Calm documentary realism, editorial and serious, like a short film for a consultancy, not a tech ad.
Natural light, soft contrast, fine film grain, 35mm look, shallow depth of field, slow camera moves
(push-in, gentle dolly, a little handheld). Real, believable places: small office, workshop, kitchen
table, evening event at a long wooden table. Colour palette: deep night tones #0B0C0F and warm paper
tones #F6F4F0 / sand #EDE6DA, neutral greys; a single muted lavender accent (#A78BFA) only where the
brand ring appears. People shown from behind, in silhouette, hands, or out of focus — no recognisable
faces, no real persons, no celebrities. No text, no letters, no logos, no readable screens.
```

## Negative prompt (always)

```
neon, glow, lens flares, holograms, robots, sci-fi, futuristic UI, floating data streams, circuit
boards, matrix code, glowing brains, AI imagery, stock-photo smiles, handshake clichés, rockets,
light bulbs, trophies, flags, readable text, letters, logos, brand names, watermarks, distorted hands,
extra fingers, cartoon, 3D render look, oversaturated colours, fast cuts, shaky chaos
```

## Voice and sound

- **Voice:** calm German AI voice, warm and factual, mid tempo, clearly marked as AI. The credits read „Stimme: KI“.
- **Address:** „Du“, short sentences, no superlatives.
- **Subtitles:** always on, German, in a grotesque typeface close to Schibsted Grotesk.
  - white on dark scenes, ink #111316 on light scenes
  - lower third, at most 2 lines
- **Music:** reduced piano with soft strings, no trailer drama.
  - It starts sparse and restless (scenes 1–3).
  - It finds a steady pulse from scene 4.
  - It opens up gently in scenes 7 and 8.
- **Sound:** a phone vibrating quietly, keyboard, paper, later the hum of conversation at the table. Never loud.

## Fixed rules (do not change)

- **Do not name:**
  - the name of our own software
  - „Connect“
  - investors
  - real customers or their logos
- **Numbers:** only the one with a source in scene 3, nothing else.
- **No real people:** do not generate Kevin or Malin as AI people. If real footage of them exists, use it instead of scenes 5–7.
- **No technology clichés:** no robots, no glowing brains, no futuristic interfaces. KI is not shown at all; the film is about the founder and the market.
- **Legal:** mark AI-generated images and the AI voice as such in the credits.

## Deliverables

1. **Master version:**
   - format 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, about 85 s
   - stereo mix with the voice at −16 LUFS
   - subtitles as a separate SRT file
2. **9:16 cut:** 1080 × 1920, about 45 s, consisting of scenes 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 9.
   - the voice shortened to scenes 1, 3, 4 and 9
   - subtitles large in the middle
3. **Thumbnail:** the product alone on the empty table from scene 3, without text. The title goes on in editing.
